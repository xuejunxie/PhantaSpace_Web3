## Origin

PhantaSpace utility token is named "Phanton" which is derived from the light particle, photon. The basic unit of Phanton is "eV". 1 Phanton = $10^9$ eV = 1 GeV.

### Check-in to earn

One of the utility of Phanton is to be used as a currency in check-in-to-earn mechanism. For example, if the owner of a space want to attract users to visit the space, he/she can use Phanton to reward the visitor. With more visitor, the NFTs in the space will have more exposure. The more exposure, the more value of the NFTs. Another example, if the owner of a real-world space, like a restaurant/shopping mall/theme park, want to attract users to visit the space, he/she can use Phanton to reward the visitor/shoppers.

### NFT ranking

Another utility of Phanton is to be used as a measure of the importance of the NFT. For example, if a important space attrack a lot of NFTs, the owner of the NFT can deposit Phanton to their NFTs. The more Phanton the owner deposit, the more important the NFT. When two NFT colide, the one with more Phanton will be the shown in the space. On the other hand, the owner of the space can manage the NFTs in their space with the measure of Phanton. THe space owner can set a minimum Phanton requirement to display the NFTs. The space owner can also charge a recurent fee based on the Phanton deposit. This way, the contents in PhantaSpace can be curated by the space owners in a decentralized way. The space owenr can use the earned Phanton to attract users by check-in-to-earn mechanism. This way, the space will benefit NFT owners to have more exposure of their NFTs. Reciprocally, the space owner attract more high value NFTs and collect more fees, and the NFT owners gain more exposure of their NFTs by deposite more Phantons.
