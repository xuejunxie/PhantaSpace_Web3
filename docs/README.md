# Welcome to PhantaSpace

![](images/banner.png)

## What's PhantaSpace?

PhantaSpace is a mixed reality metaverse infrastructure layer that sits on top of the real-world map. It enables artists and brands to create NFT galleries and games with mixed reality user experiences, thus bridging the digital assets and physical worlds.

We provide:

1. Web-based XR Metaverse codeless & one click building tools.
2. XR Metaverse content distribution hosting service.
3. XR Metaverse marketing platform: https://phanta.space

Here are some features:

- An earth-scale NFT gallery. NFT artists or collectors can place their artworks on the earth-scale 3D world map.
- Players could see those arts in the real world with AR devices - mobile AR app to start with and AR glasses in the future.
- We believe in decentralization, and therefore we offer space NFT where users can own and govern the space in PhantaSpace.
- Spaces can be addressed precisely in 3D with our unique 3D geocode and also split spaces into any scale for flexible ownership.
- Furthermore, players could pay flexible pricing for the space NFT using our on-chain auction based minting.
- Players could earn our Phanton utility token with Check-in-to-Earn feature. Space owners and NFT promoters can attract viewers by depositing Phanton in the space as reward.
- NFT promoters can stake Phanton to bid for a space for the NFT gallery.
- Space owners can profit from the space by collecting rent from deposited Phanton on the NFT in their space.
- Users will be able to buy NFTs directly on PhantaSpace through our Marketplace.

In the near future, players will be able to play the game in the real world. For example, they could
collect NFT while traveling the world, and meet up with friends from the same NFT group. People online and in the real world can coexist in a location in mixed reality. How fun is that! Eventually, PhantaSpace will grow to be a multiverse for AR content. Anyone can have his own dimension and own world.
