## What's PhantaSpace?

PhantaSpace is a showplace and marketplace. NFT artist or collectors can place their artworks in our earth-scale metaverse 1:1 to the 3D world map. The fun part is that users can see those arts in the real world with AR devices including a mobile AR app or AR glasses in the future. In order to make it decentralized, we created space NFT where users can own the space in our metaverse. Our unique 3D geocode system enables the user to split the space into any scale for flexible ownership. Our first-of-the-kind on-chain auction smart contract allows the user to start the auction for any space that enables flexible pricing for the space NFT minting. In order to bootstrap the community, we created a check-in-to-earn mechanism with our Phanton utility token. Space owners and NFT promotors can attract viewers by staking Phanton and rewarding visitors. NFT promotors can stake Phanton to bid for a position. Space owners can collect rent from stacked Phanton on the NFT in their space. Real-world retailers can attract visitors by stacking Phanton or profit from their traffic by owning the space.
