# ♾️ Multiverse

PhantaSpace is a multiverse. The earth is the base of consensus. Games and NFT developers can develop in their own orthogonal dimensions. In the time of the mixed reality future, the world is your screen. The dimension is to mixed reality metaverse as the website is to internet. Users can have their own dimensions. Users can subscribe to desired dimensions. If two objects collide at the same position from different dimensions, the content will be ranked by the Phanton value deposited on the space occupied by the object. The expected outcome of this mechanism is that, the object with the highest quality will be shown above other objects. Check out NFT ranking on [phanton.md](../token-owner-guide/phanton.md "mention").
