# ♾ Multiverse

The philosophy of PhantaSpace is a multiverse. The earth is the base of consensus. Different games and NFT developers can develop in their own orthogonal dimensions. Users will have the freedom to choose which dimension to subscribe to. In the time of the mixed reality future, the world is your screen, and the dimension is the website for web3. The purpose of PhantaSpace is to create a set of rules that everyone agrees on, decentralize the ownership, and benefit the community.



The multiverse dimension system is still in construction.
