# 👓 Mixed Reality

## Grand vision

The PhantaSpace is an open metaverse built top of 3D earth map. This enables the creation of a virtual world that is in parallel with the real world before the dawn of mixed reality. All the NFTs and virtual contents placed in PhantaSpace will be visible to all the users in the real world. At first, it's just a website, then it will be an augmented reality (AR) mobile app, and finally it will be a foundamental digital layer build into the augmented reality glasses operating system.

## PhantaField AR glasses

In parallel of PhantaSpace, our team is working on the PhantaField AR glasses. PhantaField AR glasses are consumer grade AR glasses that built with our proprietary van der Waals semiconductor (i.e. 2D semiconductor) technology. Our state of the art semiconductor technology will be able to integrate microLED and logic circuits into the glasses. It will have a form factor close to the Ironman glasses and provide large field of view (FOV) and retina resolution pixel per degree (PPD).
